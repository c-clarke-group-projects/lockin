# Worklog

Weekly progress notes. WBS 1.1.1. Owner Keshan, written in the weekly meeting with the
whole team present.

Rules:

- 1 entry per week, newest at the top, added in the weekly meeting and not later.
- Record what actually happened, not what was planned. A week where little moved is useful
  information.
- A decision made in the meeting goes in the `PLAN.md` decision log on the same day. Put a
  pointer to it here, not the decision itself.
- Blocked work needs a name next to it and a date it will be looked at again.

Entry format:

```
## Week N, dates
Done:
Not done, and why:
Blocked:
Decisions:
Next week:
```
